### The Real Product

Status isn't building nightclub software. We're building the **social credit score for physical spaces.**

Think about it: We have real-time behavioral data on the highest-spending demographic in the most social-proof-driven environment that exists. Every Status transaction is a signal of social status, spending power, and cultural influence.

The killer idea: **Status Score** - a dynamic social credit rating that venues use for real-time pricing, access, and experience personalization.

Your Status Score factors:
- Spend velocity (how much, how often)  
- Social proof (who you're with, group dynamics)
- Venue loyalty (cross-venue behavior)
- Cultural influence (early adopter patterns)

Venues use it for:
- Dynamic cover pricing (high scorers pay less or skip lines)
- Automatic table upgrades
- Personalized drink recommendations  
- VIP access without promoter gatekeeping

This isn't just "spend more get more" - it's behavioral prediction. The person who drops $200 solo on a Tuesday has different value than someone who spends $200 in a group on Saturday. We can detect and reward the cultural influencers before they become Instagram famous.

China's social credit system but for cool people. And venues pay us to implement it because it drives revenue.

**This is the moat.** Posh can copy our POS. They can't copy 18 months of behavioral data across multiple markets.

The ultimate end game: Status Score becomes the FICO score for experience economy. Hotels, restaurants, events, travel - everyone wants to know who the real high-value customers are before they walk in the door.

We're not building software. We're building infrastructure for the future of social commerce.
